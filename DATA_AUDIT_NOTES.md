# Course Compass — Course-data audit notes

## Scope and structural checks

The recommendation catalogue currently contains **52 programme records**. The code checks that programme names are unique, required fields are present, and any numeric JAMB reference cut-off is within the 0–400 range. These checks validate structure only; they do not prove that every degree award, duration, subject combination or cut-off is current.

## Changes made during the reliability review

- Clinical Sciences sub-departments such as Internal Medicine, Surgery, Paediatrics, Psychiatry and Community Medicine are no longer offered as if each were a separate undergraduate degree programme. The recommendation catalogue now uses the undergraduate **Medicine and Surgery (MBBS)** programme instead.
- The Medicine and Surgery record retains the project's specified seven-year duration. Its JAMB cut-off is left **unknown (`None`)**, so the system does not award a cut-off bonus or state an unverified number. The user-facing explanation tells the student to confirm the current requirement with ATBU and JAMB.
- Urban & Regional Planning is excluded from the recommendation catalogue in accordance with the project's stated scope. This is an explicit project-scope decision, **not** a claim that ATBU has no department by that name; ATBU's official site lists Urban and Regional Planning under Environmental Technology.
- Other stored degree-award labels, durations, subject combinations and reference cut-offs remain subject to programme-by-programme verification before the app is used as an official admissions guide.
